# Privacy Policy — X Image Viewer

_Last updated: 2026-09-30_

X Image Viewer does **not** collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension does with data

- It runs only on `https://x.com/*` and `https://twitter.com/*`, and only acts inside X's full-screen image viewer.
- When you choose **Copy**, **Region copy** or **Download**, the image you are viewing is fetched from X's own image CDN (`pbs.twimg.com`) at original size, then written to **your local clipboard** or saved to **your Downloads folder**.

## Network requests

The only requests the extension makes are to `pbs.twimg.com` for the image you asked to copy or download, sent without cookies. No request is ever sent to the developer or to any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking service.

## Storage

The extension keeps nothing on disk and uses no browser storage.
